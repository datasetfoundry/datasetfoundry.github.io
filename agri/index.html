Synthetic data · Agriculture (Ghana only, today)

Farm-monitoring data is scarcer still. Same forge, a new kind of record.

Real plot-level yield and climate-risk data almost never leaves the agency or NGO that collected it \u2014 not for students, not for methods research. Agriculture is Dataset Foundry's fourth line family, and its first line, Crop Yield, is deliberately agronomic rather than insurance/finance/health-shaped: no policy, loan, or clinical-workup window, no name field, and the record's own severity structure (single-stage classification vs. two-stage classification-plus-severity) is a per-generation choice, the same runtime toggle Breast Cancer introduced. Unlike every other line on this platform, this one doesn't yet take a country parameter at all \u2014 every record is Ghana today, honestly, not a selectable default.

Relationships tested and confirmed in every batch

These aren't hidden in the noise — they're the reason the line exists. Two hypotheses drive shortfall probability and hold no matter which stage mode you generate in. Two more, verified only in two-stage mode and scoped to shortfall plots, drive yield-loss severity.

Shortfall probability — both stage modes

Rainfall deviation from zone seasonal norm

Higher shortfall probability (deeper deficit)

The single most consistently documented driver of rainfed-crop yield variability across the agronomy literature — carries the widest loading spread in the shortfall coefficient table.

Soil-moisture deficit at critical growth stage

Higher shortfall probability

Water stress specifically during flowering/grain-fill is repeatedly identified as the single most yield-critical window for staple cereals — a real, separate driver from total-season rainfall deviation.

Yield-loss severity — two-stage mode only, shortfall plots

Rainfall-deficit magnitude

Higher yield-loss severity

Carries through mechanically from stage one — a plot that missed its shortfall threshold by a wide margin didn't just “have a shortfall,” it had a worse one.

Pest/disease outbreak

Higher yield-loss severity

Drought-stressed crops are well documented to be more susceptible to pest and disease pressure — West Africa's Fall Armyworm spread across maize since 2016 is the concrete regional instance of this compounding pattern.

How it works today

Configurable length, configurable shape

Pick a record count, a seed, and a stage mode (single-stage shortfall-only, or two-stage with severity fields \u2014 two-stage is the default), and the deterministic model runs server-side and hands you a CSV, manifest, and citation block. Same seed-and-cite reproducibility as every insurance, finance, and health line.
